About Us

From the collective,
for the collective.

Confluence FC didn’t come from football. It came from friendship.

A group of mates from the Confluence collective — more watchers than players — decided to change that in 2024. The conversation shifted from “wouldn’t it be cool” to “let’s actually do it.”

We built the squad from scratch. Designed the kit. Registered the club. And within a year of that first conversation, we stepped out onto the NZCFL Championship pitch for the first time.

None of us had played in an organised league before. No experience, no history — just a shared love of the game and the collective behind us.
